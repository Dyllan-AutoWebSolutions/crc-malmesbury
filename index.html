Our Mission

To win the lost at any cost. We mend the nets so the catch will be great, training and releasing every member for ministry.

Our Vision

Building one church in many locations. A wave of love invading every community we touch, nationally and beyond.

Our Values

Fuelled by the love of God. We are an outreach into new territory, evangelising, discipling, and caring for our community.
